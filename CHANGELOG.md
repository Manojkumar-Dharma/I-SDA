Detail lives in `git log` (each version has a matching commit); features in

## 0.10.79 and later — Keyword-compliance audit (I-series) and follow-ups

- **0.10.210** — I-121 (Cross-level; KEEP/ALWROL/CLRL/SLNO/ASSUME slice, a well-scoped piece of the "plain/base record" remainder rather than the whole undertaking): covers the existing `keepMutexConflictReason` (I-28) and `alwrolClrlSlnoConflictReason` (I-37) functions' own rule webs. Five new `RECORD_TYPES` entries in `keywordSpec.js` reuse the `mutex`/`isMutex` shape WINDOW/PULLDOWN introduced, even though none of these five is a record-type identifier (they're ordinary toggleable flags): `KEEP` (ALWROL/CLRL/SLNO), `ALWROL`/`CLRL`/`SLNO` (identical ASSUME/SFL/SFLCTL/USRDFN list each, KEEP modeled once on the `KEEP` entry instead of repeated), and `ASSUME` (deliberately narrowed to just ALWROL/CLRL/SLNO, not the fuller six-keyword DDS Reference list - SFL/USRDFN have no reachable reverse UI transition and USRDSPMGT is a separate S36E concern, matching the existing function's own pre-existing scope). New `KeywordSpec.mutexKeywords(recordType)` accessor alongside `isMutex`, for the two call sites that need the actual conflicting names to join into a message. Both writer functions now delegate to the spec instead of their own hand-written arrays. New `i121KeepAlwrolClrlSlnoAssumeKeywordSpec.test.js`: spec-vs-DDS-Reference checks for all five entries (including the ASSUME narrowing), full 186-keyword sweeps from every angle each function supports, and spot checks on the ALWROL<->ASSUME reciprocal pair. Pure refactor, no behavior change. Remaining for I-121: the rest of the plain/base record and file levels - the largest and least closed-form piece, still not split into smaller pieces. Full suite: 156 files, 9,977 checks, zero failures.
